# Post-v1 capabilities

Memories may add photos, journals, actual expenses, a photo map and timelines. Introduce S3-compatible object storage only when photos exist; PostgreSQL retains metadata.

Community may add public profiles/trips, recommendations, reports and photos. Reporting, moderation and explicit visibility controls must accompany publication; none are v1 requirements.

Collaboration may introduce trip members, permissions and shared editing. Notifications may cover advisory/entry changes and trip disruptions. Dedicated infrastructure requires actual delivery and scale requirements.

Recommendations require sufficient product data. Optional AI may support natural-language queries, semantic search or sourced summaries; the product remains fully useful without it. Native apps, bookings and commercial providers remain separate future decisions.
