# Vision

World Observatory is an interactive world intelligence, travel planning and personal exploration platform: explore + understand + plan + track + remember.

The globe is the main interaction surface, not a background. Users explore geography and thematic layers, open source-aware destination intelligence, decide whether and when to go, plan journeys, record visits and eventually retain memories.

The initial research comes from a personal objective to visit 195 countries while exploring significant territories, regions and exceptional geographic destinations. Plans cover Europe, Asia, Africa, North/Central/South America, Caribbean, Oceania and polar destinations, including the geographic poles. Research is not yet present in this repository.

The differentiator is one consistent geographic model connecting geography, live intelligence, personal history, planning, curated knowledge and provenance. Flags, generic country facts and colouring visited countries are useful foundations, not the central innovation.

The product should feel like a personal operating system for exploration. Preserve graceful degradation, distinguish data classes, minimize personal information and justify every technology by the problem it solves. It must remain useful without AI.
